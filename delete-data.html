Delete Your Data

Genvoice · Last updated 26 July 2026

Most of your data never leaves your phone. Your invoices, receipts, customer details and business information are stored only on your own device, so you can delete them yourself at any time without contacting us.

Delete everything from inside the app

  1. Open Genvoice
  2. Go to the Settings tab
  3. Scroll to the Data section
  4. Tap Clear all documents
  5. Type DELETE to confirm

This permanently removes every invoice and receipt from your device. It cannot be undone, and we hold no copy to restore.

Delete everything by uninstalling

Uninstalling Genvoice removes its entire storage from your device, including your business profile, logo, bank details, saved documents and preferences.

Request deletion of subscription records

If you have subscribed to Premium, a small amount of data is held outside your device to manage that subscription. To request its deletion, email support@loukama.com from the address linked to your Google Play account, with the subject "Data deletion request".

We will confirm and action the request within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Data Where it lives On request
Invoices, receipts, customers, business profile, logo, bank details Your device only You delete this yourself; we never receive it
Anonymous subscriber ID and purchase history RevenueCat Deleted
App version and device type used for updates Expo Deleted
Payment and transaction records Google Play Retained by Google as required by tax and accounting law. Contact Google Play support.

Deleting your account

Genvoice has no accounts. There is no sign-up, no login and no profile held on our servers, so there is no account to delete.
