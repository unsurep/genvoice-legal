Privacy Policy for Genvoice

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Genvoice ("the app") is an invoicing and receipt application published by Loukama Tech Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

In short: your invoices, receipts, customer details and business information are stored only on your own device. We do not run an account system, we cannot see your documents, and we do not sell or share your data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in its private storage on your phone. This information is never transmitted to us:

  • Business details — business name, address, logo image, and the bank name, account number and account name you choose to show on invoices.
  • Customer details — the customer name, phone number and email address you type onto a document.
  • Documents — the invoices and receipts you create, including line items and amounts.
  • Preferences — currency, VAT rate, selected template, and light or dark theme.

You can delete all of this at any time from Settings → Data → Clear all documents, or by uninstalling the app, which removes its storage entirely.

Information handled by third parties

The app uses a small number of services that process limited data:

  • Google Play Billing — if you buy a Premium subscription, the payment is processed by Google. Google tells the app only whether your subscription is currently active. We never receive or store your card or payment details, and no separate billing company is involved. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo Updates — used to deliver app updates. It receives the app version and basic device information. See Expo's Privacy Policy.

Photos and files

If you add a business logo, the app asks permission to open your photo library. Only the image you select is used, and it is stored on your device. The app does not read, scan or upload any other photos.

Sharing documents

When you export a PDF or send a document through WhatsApp, email or another app, that sharing is started by you and handled by the app you choose. We are not involved in the transfer and keep no copy.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • We do not collect analytics or usage tracking.
  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not collect your location or contacts.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

Data retention

Because your documents are stored only on your device, we hold nothing to retain or delete on your behalf. Subscription and payment records held by Google are kept according to Google's own policies.

Children

Genvoice is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app begins handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@loukama.com.
